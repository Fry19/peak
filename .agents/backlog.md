- 🟢 verified · 2026-10-22 · Codex review of Claude/walk-orbit-ground · PeakCore/Sources/PeakCore/TrailWalk.swift (`raised`) — The camera's line of sight to the walker (following and looking around) samples the ground bilinearly every ~15 m, while the mesh splits squares along the higher diagonal, so a sharp diagonal ridge can rise above what's checked: Codex's synthetic case rendered a ridge at 200 m while a sightline at 136 m passed.
