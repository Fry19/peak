# Peak backlog

Out-of-scope findings from implementation and peer review belong here. Check entries for the files a task touches; remove an entry only after fixing and verifying it. See [peer collaboration procedures](skills/peer-collaboration/SKILL.md#backlog-and-task-log) for the format.

- 🟡 verified · 2026-10-22 · Claude/satellite-default · Peak/Views/RootView.swift:126 — `tapArea()` on a Button's or Menu's label doesn't widen what's tappable — in the simulator, taps 4–6 pt outside the walk's 28 pt back button and the map's 30 pt style menu did nothing while center taps worked (both now use a 44 pt frame with `contentShape`). Other label uses likely have the same small target: RootView.swift:268/279/289, ViewshedView.swift:141, TrailFromAboveView.swift:1187 (Camera on the map's chip); StatusPill.swift:92 and RootView.swift:50 untested. Fix `TapArea` itself, or give those labels a 44 pt frame and compensate the surrounding padding; check each by tapping just outside the visible shape.
- 🟡 uncertain · 2026-10-22 · Claude/lidar-terrain · Peak/Model/DataStore.swift:352 — Copernicus tile headers are kept indefinitely and blocks fetched later aren't tied to the header's version; if the bucket replaced a file in place, new blocks would use stale offsets (decode failures, or mixed versions). Codex verified the mechanism for USGS's mutable `/current/` files, fixed there (ETag kept with the header, If-Match on block requests, 412 forgets the tile); Copernicus files are named per release, so replacement is unlikely and wasn't reproduced. Fix: use the same `versions`/If-Match/`forget` path for `.copernicus`.
- 🟢 verified · 2026-10-22 · Claude/lidar-terrain · Peak/Model/DataStore.swift:153 — Downloaded terrain blocks (`Ground/` for USGS, `DEM/` for Copernicus) accumulate as you travel; only deleting an offline area (Copernicus) or Clear downloads removes them. A 10 km visit in the US adds 30 USGS blocks (~16 MB on disk). Fix: prune blocks outside saved areas and the current place, oldest first, past a size budget.
- 🟢 verified · 2026-10-22 · Claude/lidar-terrain · Peak/Model/AppModel.swift:1280 — Offline areas don't save USGS bare ground, so without a signal inside an area, hike climbs and the maps use Copernicus unless a visit cached the blocks. Fix: fetch ground within a bounded radius of the area (25 MB per 10 km of radius) as an optional step.
- 🟢 verified · 2026-10-22 · Claude/matcher-ambiguity · Peak/Model/AppModel.swift:354 — Debug only: launched with `PEAK_TEST_PHOTO`, the camera permission prompt still appears over the labeled photo (`/tmp/peak-review/claude-matcher-ambiguity/sim-photo-portrait.png`, fresh temp simulator, iOS 26.5), though the hook says the camera is not started. Likely `RootView.swift:102` calling `setActive(true)` on `.active`, which starts sensing. - 🟡 verified · 2026-10-22 · Claude/walk-orbit-ground · Peak/Views/TrailWalkView.swift (WalkPlayer.turn/zoom) — Looking around the paused walk in 3D is drag- and pinch-only: VoiceOver users have no way to turn, tilt or zoom the camera (SceneKit's camera control before had none either). Add accessibility actions on the scene view (turn left/right, look down/up, closer/further, back behind the walker) calling `turn(by:)`/`zoom(by:)`, available while paused, and check them with VoiceOver.
- 🟢 verified · 2026-10-22 · Codex review of Claude/walk-orbit-ground · PeakCore/Sources/PeakCore/TrailWalk.swift (`raised`) — The camera's line of sight to the walker (following and looking around) samples the ground bilinearly every ~15 m, while the mesh splits squares along the higher diagonal, so a sharp diagonal ridge can rise above what's checked: Codex's synthetic case rendered a ridge at 200 m while a sightline at 136 m passed. The camera's own spot is safe (`highest(within:)` uses grid corners); only the walker's visibility is approximate. Sample the triangle actually drawn (or the higher of the bilinear and the square's diagonal heights) and add a diagonal-ridge test.
- 🟡 verified · 2026-10-22 · Claude/align-on-camera · Peak/Views/OverlayView.swift:225 — the camera view's peak labels aren't accessibility elements (no accessibilityElements in OverlayCanvas or CameraScreenView), so VoiceOver can't read or tap them; the Peaks list is the only way in. Fix: expose each `labelHits` entry as a UIAccessibilityElement (name, elevation, distance) whose activation calls `select(id)`. Note CameraScreenView is now one element while paused (its Move lines actions); fold those actions into the container when labels become elements.
- 🟡 uncertain · 2026-10-22 · Claude/walk-ring · Peak/Views/TrailFromAboveView.swift (loadImagery) — The map's own imagery is up to 3,000 px over the whole trail at 2 px a point, so for a trail frame past ~15 km a point covers more than `MapImagery.aerialMetersPerPoint` (12.5 m at 35.5°, 10.6 m at 46°) and Apple draws its coarser, greener mosaic, while the close-up tier zoomed in is its aerial photos: the colors would change zooming in. Not measured on a long trail. Load it at scale 1 when its meters a point exceed the limit (as the walk's mid tier does), or match the close-up to it, and compare on a 20+ km hike.
